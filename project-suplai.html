Decisions (5)

Five decisions that moved work off the user.

Each decision answers a pressure from the market, and each one takes a step away from the people using the product.

Trust
Verifiedተረጋግጧል

Check licenses before anyone gets in.

Buyers can't vet sellers they've never met, and regulators need to trust everyone. Verification happens once, at the door. After that, every badge in the marketplace means the same thing.

Negotiation

Inquiry, not checkout.

Pharma deals here are negotiated. A cart with fixed prices fights how people trade.

Payments

Ship what works today.

Integrated payments weren't cleared yet. Receipt upload shipped in V1, with the processor ready behind it.

Compliance

The tax record writes itself.

Compliance usually means extra work, and extra work gets skipped. Every transaction is structured, so the ledger fills in as deals close.

The fee

2%, with a receipt.

A new fee reads as a new tax on thin margins. Suplai's fee replaces what clerks already charge, and it's on record.

Architecture (6)

One card on top. Four layers underneath.

A wholesaler sees a product, a stock level and a price. Each layer under that card has to hold before the card can be trusted.

Verification decides who is allowed in. Access rules in the database decide what each business can read. The transaction record ties every inquiry, agreement and payment to both parties. The tax ledger turns those records into summaries without anyone filling in a form.

None of it shows up on screen. That is the point.
